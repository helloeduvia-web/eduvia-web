Frequently asked questions

Simple answers before you begin.

What do I get for ₹100?

You get a personalized assessment experience and a sample scorecard flow with profile-based university and budget guidance. In the full product, your final report will be generated from your saved profile.

Will Eduvia guarantee admission?

No. University recommendations and admission chances are estimates, not guarantees.

Can I speak to an Eduvia expert?

Yes. After completing the assessment, you can request an expert consultation. The production version will route the request into Eduvia’s counselor workflow.
