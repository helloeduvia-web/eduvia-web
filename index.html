FAQ

Simple answers before you begin.

What do I get for ₹100?

A guided assessment experience and a personalized starting report with profile-fit guidance, estimated budget and university categories. Production integrations can later connect this to live university data.

Does Eduvia guarantee admission?

No. University fit, admission chance, cost and visa outcomes are estimates and can change based on current requirements.

Can I speak with an Eduvia expert?

Yes. After completing the assessment, the student can request an expert call. The production version can route that request into the counselor CRM.
